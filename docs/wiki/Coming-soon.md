# Coming soon

Ideas we are working on or planning next. There are no dates, and plans can change.

## Hardpoint

- **Ready check.** A Ready button in the fleet panel tells the admiral and commanders you are good
  to go. Cards show a green or red dot, and units roll up as "5 of 6 ready". Works with a CIC
  relay and peer to peer.
- **More manufacturer themes** for dashboards.
- **Clearer support across game versions.** On a game build that does not take actions (4.x LIVE),
  action buttons and toggles show "needs game 5.0" instead of doing nothing, and widgets for data
  the game does not send say "not sent by this game version".
- **Companion discovery.** The game PC finds your companion laptop or tablet on the local network,
  so there is no address to type.
- **Easier hosting.** Optional automatic router setup (UPnP) for hosting a peer-to-peer fleet.
- **Ready counts on Stream Deck.** The Fleet Status plugin shows how many are ready.

## With CIC

- **Ready check** on the CIC Live board too, with the same dots and unit roll-ups.
- **Org customization in CIC:** upload your org's logo and set its colours in a settings panel.
